At GoWeb, accessible from GoWeb.in, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by GoWeb and how we use it.

3. Log Files

GoWeb follows a standard procedure of using log files. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

4. Client-Side Processing

We prioritize your privacy by designing tools that process data client-side. This means that when you use our Image Resizer, Code Minifier, or Financial Calculators, the data (images, code, numbers) is processed within your browser's memory and is generally not uploaded to our servers unless explicitly required for the tool's function.
